An interactive installation prototype for a World Cup watch party.

At Manufactured, I prepared the wraparound facade for production and designed parts for the coaster input, power supply, and servo string routing.

01 / The experience

Insert a coaster and ask Paul for a prediction.

The team designed a cabinet inspired by Paul the Octopus. The intended sequence was to insert a coaster, watch Paul move, and receive a preprinted prediction coaster.

02 / Facade

From the studio’s visual direction to a printable wrap.

Manufactured supplied the initial AI-generated visual direction. I rebuilt low-resolution elements as vectors, incorporated supplied and sourced artwork, and prepared the three panels for print. I helped align the panel openings and mounting holes with the physical frame. The final artwork covered three panels, so the flat layout and the installed facade are shown together below.

03 / Fit and motion

Revise the input around the available coasters.

I measured the coasters we had, updated the input geometry in Fusion, and reprinted parts after checking clearance and collisions. I used full-size paper templates to check fit before fabricating stainless-steel brackets.

I wired and labeled about 16 servos and helped test tentacle motion. During an early test, the taped connection between a servo and its string loosened.

04 / Build status

The facade was installed and subsystems were tested.

Coordinating the full motion and coaster sequence was still in progress when I left.
