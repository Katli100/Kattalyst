About me

I like making ideas clear, useful, and tangible.

I’m a designer based in Grandville, Michigan, with a BS in Integrated Design & Media from NYU Tandon, completed in May 2026. My work brings together physical product design, hands-on prototyping, and graphic communication.

Throughout my time in Alpha Omega Epsilon, I volunteered as the chapter’s graphic designer, creating recruitment, event, and chapter communications. At Manufactured, I prepared Illustrator facade artwork, maintained project documentation, and made photo-based client updates consistent with the studio’s branding, alongside fabrication work.

I’m especially interested in consumer tech and the small details that make it easier to use. With keyboards, that might mean swapping a switch or a plate and listening for what changed. For Cordify, it meant taking apart commercial cable retractors to understand how the ends moved.
