Event and recruitment communications for NYU’s Alpha Omega Epsilon chapter, across social graphics, flyers, print pieces, merchandise, and a semester recap.

Role
Volunteer graphic designer / Chapter Historian
Formats
Flyers, displays, cards, merchandise, recap

I created chapter graphics throughout my involvement from September 2023 to May 2026. This was volunteer work alongside my other chapter roles.

Make the event and the next step easy to find.

I designed these pieces for social, professional, fundraising, and recruitment events. The Book Drive flyer leads with the purpose, then dates and the collection point; the Red Hat panel uses a grid to separate the event, time, and location. 04 / Chapter storytelling

A semester, told through photographs.

The Spring 2024 recap brings chapter photographs, activities, and short descriptions into a scrapbook-style sequence.

A silent slideshow covering the executive board, recruitment, new members, social events, professional events, philanthropy, and graduating seniors. Pause or use the timeline to take a closer look at a page.

05 / Scope and reflection

One chapter, several communication needs.

I designed layouts, set typography, and adapted event information across social and print formats. The collection brings together recruitment, event, and chapter communications from my volunteer work.

For a professional marketing campaign, I would check the smallest text at its intended viewing size, use a simpler type hierarchy where details are dense, and confirm image rights before adapting character-led pieces for another organization.
