NYU fabrication study · Fall 2024

Cup Noodles paper model

A full-scale packaging study in paper and foil.

How I made it

I modeled a full-scale truncated cone in Rhino, unrolled its sidewall, and added tabs for tape or glue. I recreated the label from Nissin’s packaging in Illustrator and added foil to give the paper model weight and support the toppings.
