Cup Noodles paper model

  • Rhino
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Paper modeling
  • Packaging study

A full-size Cup Noodles model made from paper and foil.

How I made it

I modeled the tapered cup at full size in Rhino, unrolled the side, and added tabs for tape or glue. I recreated the Nissin label in Illustrator and used foil to give the model some weight and support the toppings.
