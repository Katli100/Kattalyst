About me

I like finding out what makes things work.

I graduated from NYU Tandon in May 2026 with a degree in Integrated Design & Media after starting in electrical engineering. I like taking products apart, comparing how they work, and turning what I find into something I can model, print, and test.

I’m especially interested in consumer tech and the small details that make it easier to use. I like comparing products and asking what makes one feel more intuitive than another. With keyboards, that might mean swapping a switch or a plate and listening for what changed. For Cordify, it meant taking apart commercial cable retractors to understand why one end couldn’t move without disturbing the other.
