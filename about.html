About me

I enjoy making things and figuring out how they work.

I graduated from NYU Tandon with a BS in Integrated Design & Media in May 2026, and I’m now based in Grandville, Michigan. I work across product and graphic design, with a particular interest in 3D modeling, prototyping, and consumer technology.

I spent my chapter years in Alpha Omega Epsilon creating flyers, recruitment materials, and other graphics as a volunteer. During my internship at Manufactured, I worked on fabrication and print artwork. I also maintained project documents and put together photo updates for clients, keeping the slides consistent with the studio’s branding.

I’m especially interested in everyday technology and how people use it. I like building keyboards, comparing components, and seeing how a change affects the feel or sound. That same curiosity shows up in my projects. For Cordify, I took apart existing cable retractors to understand how they worked before trying my own mechanisms.
