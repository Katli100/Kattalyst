03 / NYC advocacy poster

Housing for All

  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Adobe Photoshop
  • Poster design
  • Typography

“Build up, not out.” A housing advocacy message created for a New York City context.

Context
NYC advocacy design study
Subject
Upzoning
My role
Poster design and layout
Format
Single-page flyer

The background

Building upward as the visual idea.

The poster uses a vertical cityscape to reinforce its central message. The subject is upzoning, and the intended context is New York City.

Orange and navy give the central housing graphic a strong presence. The headline is split between the top and bottom of the composition, with the housing emblem at the center. A QR code is included in the original artwork as a route to more information.

Palette + typography

A compact visual language.

Navy, white, and orange form the poster’s palette. Cascadia Code Bold and Regular establish the type hierarchy, pairing a strong headline with smaller supporting information.

Design reflection

Keep the message visible at a glance.

The vertical buildings connect the subject to the phrase “Build up, not out.” The circular emblem holds the illustration and the campaign title together; the border and monospace type repeat the navy-and-orange treatment.

The work shown is a poster design study. A next production pass would check the QR destination, small-text contrast, and readability at the intended print size. This page describes the visual communication rather than claiming a policy or campaign outcome.
